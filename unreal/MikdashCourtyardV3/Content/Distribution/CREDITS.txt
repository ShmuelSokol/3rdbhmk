Architecture
This walkthrough carries forward the project's measured reconstruction based on
Yechezkel. Source revision: 41265f719bbea940f980a74aaed655a05552e489.
The retained reconstruction uses 0.5 metres per amah. Interpretive choices,
materials and illustrative surroundings are not a certification of historical
or future appearance.

Terrain
Terrain derived from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, distributed through the AWS Open Data
Registry. The inherited project terrain includes leveling and rounding; it is
not a site survey. The 748-metre reference elevation is a project datum.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Terrain provider attribution and source information:
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/data-sources.md
SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
The six source tile headers identify SRTM N31E035. Their 66,049 sampled
elevations exactly match the retained source terrain data (checked 2026-09-07).

The FLAC recordings were decoded to 16-bit, 44.1 kHz WAV without pitch, rate or
