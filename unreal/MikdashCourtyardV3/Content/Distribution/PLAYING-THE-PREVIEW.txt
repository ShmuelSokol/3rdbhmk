PLAYING THE BEIS HAMIKDASH PREVIEW

This is a development preview. The reconstruction, visuals and activities are
still being reviewed. It is not a finished experience or a halachic ruling,
and completing a lesson does not certify permission to enter a sacred area.

GET STARTED
Extract all supplied ZIP files into the same folder. Keep the extracted files
together, then open MikdashCourtyardV3.exe. Unreal Engine need not be installed.
Choose "Begin the walkthrough". To skip the introduction, wait at least one
second after it starts, then press a key.

WALK AND LOOK
W, A, S, D or the arrow keys: walk.
Mouse: look around.
P or Escape: open the pause menu and release the mouse.
Choose "Resume" to continue.

EXPLORE AS A WHITE DOVE
Stand on clear ground, then press F or choose "Explore as a white dove" in
the menu. Use the movement keys and mouse to explore; Space rises, Ctrl
descends, and Shift flies faster.
Press F again, or choose "Return to walking", to return to your original
departure point. This does not land you beneath the bird's current position.

OPTIONAL PREPARATION LESSON
Choose "Preparation lesson (architectural review)" from the menu and select
a fictional story. Read the question and answer choices; "Show a hint" helps.
Private preparation stays offscreen: no undressing or immersion is shown.
Use "Continue after the private preparation", then "Back to walkthrough menu".
The lesson does not remove outstanding requirements or grant entry permission.

FINISH
Open the menu and choose "Quit" to close the experience.
Keep the credits and third-party notices with any copy you share.
