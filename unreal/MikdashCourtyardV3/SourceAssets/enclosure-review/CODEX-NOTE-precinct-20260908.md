# Codex-facing note: the precinct entries after the 8 September 2026 decision

For the owner of `Content/Distribution/Tour/codex-entries.json` (not edited here). Proposed
replacement text for the three `precinct` entries; every claim is marked certain / disputed /
authored as the codex requires. Numbers from `precinct-Main50.json` / `precinct-Candidate48.json`.

## `precinct` (category note)

The level opens in the YECHEZKEL view: the sacred precinct built exactly as Yechezkel 42:15-20
states it — five hundred reeds a side, a reed of six amot (40:5), so 3,000 amot — with a wall a
reed thick and a reed high and five gates. At the book's own amah (48 cm, recovered from its
"about 1,440 m" on 42:16) that is 1.44 km a side; at the main map's 50 cm scale, 1.5 km. The
square covers roughly the eastern two fifths of today's Old City — its west wall stands about
170–180 m west of the Kotel — and, east and south of the Old City walls, the Kidron valley, the
lower slope of the Mount of Olives, the City of David and Silwan. **The modern buildings inside
it are hidden, never deleted**: one press shows today's city again (MODERN), a second draws the
boundary over the standing city (OVERLAY).

## `precinct-wall-and-gates` — confidence: certain (wall, count, opening); authored (positions, ground)

* Wall 6 x 6 amot round all four sides — certain (Yechezkel 40:5; Mishkenei Elyon 196 m.2).
* Five gates, two south, one each east, north, west; opening 10 wide x 50 high, as thick as the
  wall — certain (Mishkenei Elyon 196 m.2; book pp. 115-117). Sixty amot total with piers — the
  author's starred suggestion (authored).
* WHERE the gates stand along each wall is not in the sources (authored): east, north and west
  on the Temple's own axes; the east gate at world Y 0 is the axis of the court's east gate and of
  the visitor's walking start, so the approach from the Mount of Olives passes through it. The two
  south gates at a third and two thirds from the south-west corner. Replace the "As built" line
  with: main map E [116900, 0], N [0, −33150], W [−33100, 0], S1 [16900, 116850],
  S2 [66900, 116850]; candidate E [112224, 0], N [0, −31824], W [−31776, 0], S1 [16224, 112176],
  S2 [64224, 112176]. Receipts: `SourceAssets/enclosure-review/precinct-Main50.json`,
  `precinct-Candidate48.json`, `geometry-manifest.json`.
* Thresholds follow the ground: the east gate stands on the Mount of Olives slope at about
  784 m a.s.l., 36 m above the Temple platform, with the Kidron between it and the Mount; the
  north gate at 743 m; the west gate at 751 m; the two south gates at 684 and 694 m in the
  Kidron/Silwan slopes (authored: the sources place no gate on any contour).
* The wall crosses the Kidron on its east and south sides, dropping 100–200 m. The verse gives
  the wall a section and the book a height; neither says what carries it down a valley. The
  substructure drawn under it (up to ten metres under single 12.5 m modules on the gorge bank)
  is this project's construction — authored, and said so.
* Hidden inside the wall on the main map: 1,914 of the 11,437 OSM buildings by centroid (486 of
  the 2,106 detailed Old City facades), as 279 batched actors; 1,700 / 270 on the candidate. A
  100 m batch the wall line cuts is hidden whole if any of its buildings is inside, so some
  buildings just outside the wall vanish with their batch; listed per cell in the receipts.
  Nothing is deleted.
