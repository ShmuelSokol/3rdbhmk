# Root transform contract
Mesh/instance origin, not pelvis or bounds centre, is the pivot. Root coordinates are centimetres with source conversion (x,z,y)*100. Retain baked bob and arms. Only yaw and positive uniform scale are covered; scale radius and both signed Z limits exactly once. World velocity drift is added after scale. Different root trajectories require enclosing both; no additional WPO, pitch, roll, or nonuniform scale is certified. Negative lower bounds are retained and do not alone prove ground penetration. Bounds are not controller/environment collision acceptance.
