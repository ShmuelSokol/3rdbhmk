A 40-second preview of the current Beis HaMikdash Unreal scene: aerial overview,
courtyard, Kotel area, Heikhal and Aron. This is work in progress, not a finished game
or a claim of final historical/halachic accuracy.

Download the attached MP4 to save or share it from your phone. The video is silent,
7.8 MB, H.264, 24 fps; 896x504 rendered frames are upscaled to 1280x720. No game
installation or GitHub account is needed to download the public video.

Lighting, vessels, crowds and surrounding terrain still need work. The video shows
the current main map; the approved exact-48-cm candidate has not yet replaced it.
The existing Windows game download is unchanged by this video-only release.
