## What the user asked for and what this delivers

"Tons of people getting on and off at each stop" and "lots of people taking pictures".
`AMikdashTransit` already fires `OnRequestAlighting` / `OnRequestBoarding` at every
doors-open edge with `(GlobalStopIndex, Count, BoardingPoint, SecondsAvailable, RouteId)`,
and `SuggestPhotographerCount(stop, count, run)` answers about 22 percent. Nothing consumed
them. `AMikdashTransitBoardingBridge` does:

- **Alighting**: `Count` figures step off at this arrival's door (kerb side of the door plane,
  never inside the vehicle body), released one per door every 0.8 s inside the first 45
  percent of the dwell, walk 6–14 m onto the kerb-side apron and wait for their companions;
  `SuggestPhotographerCount` of them instead walk 3–5 m, turn to face the Mount
  (`MountFocusCm`) and stand 4–9 s before leaving. Non-photographers leave together as one
  party (or are handed to the crowd, below).
- **Boarding**: `Count` figures are already waiting on a jittered lattice 1.1–5.2 m off the
  door plane along the platform face; each leaves at the moment that puts it through the
  door in its own door slot, from 45 percent of the dwell onward, and **the last one is
  through the door at least `CloseMarginSeconds` (1 s) before the dwell ends**. A figure that
  cannot make it is not animated. A boarder still walking when the doors close is counted as
  an overrun (`GetOverrunCount`), which the math makes zero by construction.
- **Sizing over the dwell**: a group is `min(requested, door throughput in its phase,
  MaxPeoplePerGroup 40, free figures)`; e.g. a 20 s rail dwell with a request of 70
  animates 24 boarders and 22 alighters; a 14 s bus dwell with 26 requested animates 8 and 7.
  The trimmed remainder is counted (`GetPeopleTrimmed`), not hidden.
- **Concurrency cap**: `MaxConcurrentGroups` (default **6**, config) across all sixteen
  stops; an alighting and a boarding group at one arrival are two groups. Beyond the cap a
  group is refused whole (`GetGroupsRefusedByCap`). The transit layer itself caps nothing.
- **Thin crowd**: the figure pool (`MaxFigures`, default 240 = 6 × 40) is shared; a group is
  sized down to what is free and refused only below `MinPeoplePerGroup`.
- **Never across a keep-out**: every planned walk is sampled every 25 cm against the crowd's
  runtime keep-outs plus `ProtectedMarginCm`; inside an authored crowd zone the crowd's own
  `IsTransitSegmentAllowed` is consulted as well; one 34/96 capsule sweep per walk against
  static blockers (the crowd groups' convention). Figures only ever move along their
  accepted straight segment; nothing is relocated after planning.
- **Never inside a vehicle**: the apron is the half-plane beyond the door plane toward the
  platform face (`GetStopFurniturePoint`); every point must sit ≥ `DoorClearanceCm` (60) on
  the kerb side. Lattice points are provably ≥ 85 cm, fan points ≥ 0.34 × 600 = 205 cm.
- **Off-route stops refused**: `GetStopProjectionErrorCm` is read for every stop after the
  transit actor has built its routes (lazily at first arrival if the bridge starts first);
  a stop over `MaxStopProjectionErrorCm` (250) never gets a group (`GetRefusedStopIndices`).
- **Deterministic**: everything about a group hashes from `(Seed, GlobalStopIndex, RunIndex,
  direction)`. `RunIndex` comes from the rich `OnPassengerExchange` payload, which the same
  `BroadcastBoarding` call fires immediately before the two legacy hooks; the bridge binds
  all three and matches by (stop, count, door point). If only the legacy hooks fire, a
  per-stop arrival counter stands in and the plan is still deterministic per arrival.
- **Cost**: figures are HISM instances of the crowd's own pose meshes (one component per
  pose, plus one for an optional `PhotographerPoseMesh`), custom data 0/1/2 = gait phase /
  garment tint / standing flag exactly as `AMikdashCrowdField` writes them, so the crowd
  material works unchanged. Deadlines are checked for every figure every frame (≤ 240);
  movement is round-robin under `UpdateBudget` (64) with catch-up integration.

## Design choice: a placed actor, not a world subsystem

Every runtime system in this plugin is an opt-in placed actor with `bActivateOnBeginPlay
= false`, placed by a guarded, checkpointed release script and read back from the saved
map. A `UWorldSubsystem` would (a) come alive in every world — the protected maps, the
isolated 48 cm candidate, editor preview worlds — violating the project's opt-in rule,
(b) have nothing to checkpoint, save, reopen or read back, so no receipt could prove its
configuration, and (c) have nowhere to hold per-map data such as `MountFocusCm`, which
differs between the legacy 50 cm main map and the 48 cm candidate. The actor finds exactly
one `AMikdashTransit` and one `AMikdashCrowdField` at start (`bAutoFindActors`) or uses
the references the release script set, and refuses on zero or several.

## Two findings the coordinator must know

1. **No transit stop lies inside any crowd zone.** The six authored zones sit within about
   ±160 m of the Temple (OuterCourtEast, OuterCourtWestTerrace, MountPlatformDeck,
   KotelPlazaStrip, KotelApproachCorridor) plus one 22 × 13 m strip of Batei Mahase street.
   All sixteen TransitV3 stops are on Jaffa Road, HaTsanhanim, Sultan Suleiman, Ma'ale
   HaShalom, Ir David and Hativat Yerushalayim, 0.6–2.2 km from the Temple; even the
   Batei Mahase stop at `[-40767, 47267]` is 18 m outside its street zone's western edge
   (`-38800`). Consequences: there are no crowd instances near any stop to "converge", and
   `AMikdashCrowdField::IsTransitSegmentAllowed` returns **false everywhere** because it
   requires every sample to be inside a zone.
2. **The existing `AMikdashTransitCrowdCoordinator` therefore cannot render a single
   figure at the current stops.** Its `CorridorClear` calls `IsTransitSegmentAllowed` first
   and also demands a real ground-trace hit at every 25 cm sample. It is not placed in the
   main map (no receipt under `SourceAssets/runtime-review/transit-crowd/`; the placer
   `Scripts/release_transit_crowd.py` has never run). The bridge refuses to start beside a
   coordinator with `bActivateOnBeginPlay` (`bRefuseIfCoordinatorActive`), and the release
   script refuses placement in that case, because both would animate every exchange twice.
   Nothing in the coordinator was edited.

Because of (1) the bridge does **not** require zone membership: outside every zone it
applies the keep-outs (with margin) directly through `CrowdFieldMath.h`; inside a zone it
adds the crowd's own judgement. Where the ground trace misses (commandlets, NullRHI, a road
mesh without collision) the door threshold's own height is used and the miss is counted
(`GetGroundTraceMissCount`), never treated as a refusal.

## What the crowd owner must expose (nothing was edited in the crowd files)

`AMikdashCrowdField` has no public way to add, remove or relocate an instance: `Agents` is
private and `BuildCrowd` rebuilds everything. The bridge therefore defines the minimal
contract as a UInterface in its own header and probes for it with
`Cast<IMikdashCrowdPartyHost>(CrowdField)`; today the cast returns null and the bridge's own
figures leave/appear at the apron edge instead. To make alighters become real crowd parties
and boarders come out of the real waiting crowd, the crowd owner implements on
`AMikdashCrowdField` (or a subclass):

```cpp
class AMikdashCrowdField : public AActor, public IMikdashCrowdPartyHost
// FMikdashCrowdPartyRequest: AnchorCm, OutwardDirection, Count, Seed, SecondsAvailable,
//                            bStanding, FacingTarget, GlobalStopIndex   (all world cm)
bool  TryAdoptParty(const FMikdashCrowdPartyRequest& Request, TArray<int32>& OutAgentIndices);
int32 TryReleaseNearest(const FVector& Point, int32 Count, float MaxRadiusCm, TArray<FVector>& OutPositions);
bool  IsCrowdPointAllowed(const FVector& Point, float RadiusCm) const;
```

Required semantics:

- `TryAdoptParty`: create one stable party of `Count` (1 = an individual) at the anchor in
  the zone containing it, using the existing `SeedSocialZone` placement rules; **refuse
  atomically** (return false, change nothing) when the anchor is in no zone, inside a
  keep-out skirt, or the crowd is at capacity. The bridge calls it once per alighting
  party when its last walker has arrived, and once per photographer (Count 1) when the
  photograph is done.
- `TryReleaseNearest`: remove up to `Count` valid agents within `MaxRadiusCm` of `Point`
  and in the same zone as `Point`, return their feet positions, mark the instances hidden.
  The bridge passes a radius of `min(3000, 0.45 × dwell × 90 cm/s)`, so every released
  figure provably reaches the door in time (test "guaranteed fits"). Returning 0 changes
  nothing and the bridge falls back to its lattice.
- `IsCrowdPointAllowed`: keep-out-only legality (no zone requirement), i.e. the
  `PointInAnyProtected` half of `IsTransitSegmentAllowed`.
- Independently of the interface: **authoring a crowd zone at each stop apron** (kerb side,
  ~6 × 20 m, low density, `StandingRatio` ~0.5) is what puts a waiting crowd at the stops
  at all. Without it there is nobody to converge and nothing for the coordinator to draw.

## Limitations, stated plainly

- Boarders appear on the apron when the doors open (they were not walking there earlier)
  and alighters vanish 6–14 m from the door; both are the honest consequence of the crowd
  having no hand-over API and no zone at any stop. The interface above removes both.
- Figures are the crowd's frozen stride poses; a photographer with no `PhotographerPoseMesh`
  is a stride pose held still with the standing flag, not a person raising a phone.
- Ground comes from a trace where it hits and from the door threshold's height otherwise;
  road-surface collision at the stops has not been checked by this task.
- The 48 cm candidate needs its own `MountFocusCm`; the spec targets the main map only.
- Nothing here is a claim of visual or packaged acceptance.
