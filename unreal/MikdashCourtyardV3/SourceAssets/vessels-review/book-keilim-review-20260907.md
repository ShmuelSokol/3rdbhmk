# Keilim placement review against "Lishchno Tidreshu" (book export of 2026-09-07)

Scope: read-only comparison of the Unreal scene layout (heikhal-keilim-spec.json, aron-placement-spec.json, menorah-model-spec.json) with the book's English draft (Book-Source-Data.json page-level `englishOutput`, spot-checked against the stored Hebrew `hebrewInput`). Page numbers are source-PDF page indexes (the book's own printed folio is often ~105 lower, e.g. PDF 241 = printed 136).

Caveats (from READ-ME.txt / translation-coverage.json): all 363 translated pages are DRAFT; pages 68 and 85 are known translation failures (neither is relevant to keilim); pages 1, 365-367 have no text. The book's placement summary ("Ketz HaYamin") is repeated verbatim on PDF pp. 241, 243, 245, 247, 249, 251, 253, 255, 257 (running header of the pasuk 41:22 section), so the repetition is not independent confirmation. Diagrams could not be inspected: the PDF (605 MB) exceeds the Read tool's 100 MB limit and no PDF renderer (PyMuPDF/pypdf/Ghostscript/mutool) is installed, so where "edge vs centre" matters the book's diagram 22:17 (PDF p. 250), 22:22 (p. 253) and 22:29 (p. 258) should be opened by hand.

Scene frame used below: X east, Y south, Z up, 50 cm/amah. Heichal clear floor X -5600..-3600 (40 amot; entrance face at -3600, west/KhK wall face at -5600), Y -500..+500 (20 amot). Kodesh HaKodashim floor X -6700..-5700, Y -500..+500 (20 x 20 amot), 2-amah (100 cm) wall between them at X -5700..-5600. Heichal mid-line X = -4600; literal "eastern third" line X = -4267.

## 3. Menorah branch orientation

Book: adopts Rambam — lamps/branches run north-south (pp. 252-254), and it explicitly draws the Rashi alternative (east-west) as diagram 22:23a. Wicks: six toward the centre lamp, centre lamp toward the KhK (Rambam 3:8, p. 252).

Classic dispute (Menachos 98b, baraisa on the tables and the menorah): "מזרח ומערב היו מונחין, דברי רבי; רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון אומר צפון ודרום" — Rabbi (Yehudah HaNasi): east-west; R' Elazar b. R' Shimon: north-south (Sefaria text checked).
- Rambam, Beis HaBechirah 3:12: "וכך היו נרות המנורה כנגד רוחב הבית בין הצפון ובין הדרום" — branches span the width of the House, NORTH-SOUTH (Sefaria/Chabad checked). Follows R' Elazar b. R' Shimon; the "western lamp" is then the middle lamp (3:8).
- Ra'avad, gloss on 3:12 (Sefaria checked): "זהו מחלוקת התנאים וסדר התמיד אינו כן שהרי שנינו מצא שתי נרות מזרחיות דולקים וזהו לענין מזרח ומערב מונחים" — EAST-WEST, from Tamid 3:9 ("he found the two eastern lamps burning").
- Rashi: EAST-WEST (Menachos 98b s.v. מזרח ומערב; Shabbos 22b s.v. נר מערבי — the western lamp is the second from the east; Rashi Shemos 25:37 on the six wicks turned toward the centre). Rashi's exact wording was not retrieved in this pass; the book's own caption (p. 254) attributes east-west to Rashi.

Scene: branches spread north-south, full fan visible looking west from the entrance = Rambam / R' Elazar b. R' Shimon = the book's adopted position. No change.

## 4. Recommendations

1. Move shulchan to about X -5300, Y -350 (reading A) or -5350/-375 (reading B); keep yaw 0. Confidence high on the ~8-amah X correction (book pp. 241, 250, 252 are explicit and consistent in Hebrew and English); medium on the 50-75 cm Y correction pending diagram 22:17 (edge vs centre). Sources: book pp. 241, 250-252; Rashi Shemos 26:35; Menachos 98b-99a; Baraisa d'Meleches HaMishkan.
2. Move menorah to about X -5330, Y +315..+375 (Y depends on whether 2.5 amot is measured to the branch tips, the base, or the centre; +300 is already inside the plausible band, so Y can stay at +300..+350). Keep branches north-south. Add the three-step tending stone east of it (Rambam 3:11, p. 252) and orient wicks: six toward the centre lamp, centre lamp toward the west (Rambam 3:8). Confidence high for X, medium for Y.
3. Move incense altar to about X -5150, Y 0 (any X in -5250..-4700 satisfies "western half, drawn a little east of the shulchan-menorah line"; X = -4500 is wrong under the book's reading of Menachos 98b / Rambam 3:17). Confidence high that it must be west of -4600; medium on the exact value. Sources: book pp. 241, 245, 255-258; Yoma 33b; Menachos 98b; Yerushalmi Shekalim 6:3.
4. Update the spec's `basis` text: replace "beyond eastern third" with "western half of the 40-amah Heichal (= Rambam's 'third' of the 60-amah Bayis, per Menachos 98b, Yerushalmi Shekalim 6:3, Minchas Chinuch 1:7 as discussed in Lishchno Tidreshu pp. 257-258)". Cite Rashi Shemos 26:35 / Yoma 33b for the 2.5-amot clearances, not Rambam 3:12-17.
5. Aron: no change to (-6200, 0, 925) yaw 90. Keep the Rambam 3:12 citation (it is correct). Record the alternatives: Rambam 4:1 puts the stone/Aron in the west of the KhK (unquantified); Tosafos Yom Tov and Tanchuma centre it; the book leaves it open (pp. 187, 189, 306). Confidence medium. Optional: an Even HaShtiya slab raised "three fingers" (Yoma 53b, p. 187) under the Aron, 12 x 12 amot centred per the book's study suggestion (p. 189, explicitly "for study only").
6. Keruvim: keep anchors at the N and S ends of the kaporet, faces toward each other and downward toward the kaporet, wings spread up and over; body height 10 tefachim (83 cm) per the book (pp. 62, 190). Treat the book's "six wings each" as its own statement (no source given). Confidence medium.
7. Paroches: place at X = -5600 (Heichal face of the KhK wall), 7 amot wide x 6 amot high (350 x 300 cm), Y -175..+175; KhK doors 2 x (3.5 x 6 amot), always open, modelled opening inward (book's preferred option, pp. 60, 261). Confidence medium-high (Yechezkel 41:3 + Mishkenos Elyon; the paroches itself is "not mentioned in the prophet", p. 185).
8. Dimensions to flag (not placement): the book's Third-Temple shulchan is 3 amot (150 cm) high vs the scene's 75 cm (p. 241 main line; alternative reading p. 246); menorah 18 tefachim = 150 cm at this project's 50 cm amah (the spec's 180 cm is a Temple Institute conversion at a larger amah); the book says the golden altar's amah is 5 tefachim (p. 255, Eruvin 4a), i.e. ~41.7 cm square rather than 50. Single shulchan and single menorah are correct for the book's Third Temple (Shlomo's ten are described for the First Temple only, pp. 256-257).
