agent produced these dossiers. They are a source map and production specification,
not a completed simulation or a claim to know every detail of the future.

## Current production decisions

- Preserve the measured Yechezkel architecture and its coordinate convention.
- Match the selected Temple Institute manufactured objects, with actual-object
  measurements and reference views still to be obtained; do not merge competing models.
- Keep the future city distinct from the existing modern OSM comparison context.
- Use a shared calendar and persistent goal-driven identities for people.
- Teach reviewed decisions in context. Unknown rules remain review items; quiz scores
  never grant sacred permissions or measure spiritual worth.
- Show immersion through a private offscreen transition, fully dressed before/after.
- Use fresh maintained stone with restrained block-level variation and motion review.
- Start with a complete small arrival journey before scaling crowds or festivals.

## Research completeness and implementation status

Every user-requested domain has a research section and work package. Several exact
answers remain unresolved: future head counts, full urban scale/mapping, some future
service reconciliations, object measurements/variants, exact market locations and
music. No invented answer is promoted to prophecy or settled halacha. Further
source and qualified halachic review are needed before scoring disputed lessons.

Implementation has begun: a five-scenario preparation learning model, three authored resident schedules with in-memory identity/task continuity, and an unassigned procedural stone pilot. Root integrated the lesson into the paused menu and the resident clock into unpaused gameplay. Native compilation and Walkthrough-03 packaging passed; lesson UI and stone shader/visual review are pending. These are foundations: no embodied characters, physical mikvah environment, new vessels, reviewed sacred-zone permission table or complete curriculum yet.
