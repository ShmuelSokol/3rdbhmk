# Kaporet keruvim: an actionable original modeling brief

Research checked 2026-09-07. Scope is the **two keruvim arising from the Aron's kaporet**, not Shlomo's room-scale figures, wall carvings or woven curtain figures. This is a source-based interpretation brief, not an exact recovered historical shape. It does not change the existing native study assembly or claim that its reserved anchors are finished figures.

## Features with direct textual support

Exodus 25:18–20 gives two gold keruvim, one at each end of the cover, formed with it, wings raised and sheltering the cover, with faces directed toward each other and toward the cover. Rashi explains their formation from the cover rather than separate fabrication followed by attachment, and their raised wings rather than folded/resting wings. Preserve these relationships visibly. [Exodus 25:18–20 with Rashi](https://www.chabad.org/parshah/torahreading_cdo/aid/2492662/p/complete/jewish/Terumah-Torah-Reading.htm).

Sukkah 5b describes childlike faces and a face measure not less than a tefach. Its discussion derives ten tefachim above the Ark/cover for the keruvim and the covering wings, with wings slightly above their heads. The passage also discusses the differing five- and six-tefach vessel-cubit systems. Use a named profile; do not mix the arithmetic from incompatible systems. [Sukkah 5b](https://www.sefaria.org/Sukkah.5b).

For the existing study's chosen six-tefach, 50 cm amah profile, ten tefachim convert to approximately **83.33 cm**. The cover top is already approximately 83.33 cm above the body bottom. A corresponding source-guided wing-clearance datum is therefore approximately **166.67 cm above that bottom**. This is a modeling conversion and a reference datum, not a surveyed wingtip coordinate. Wing thickness, head proportions and exactly where an artist measures the surface require explicit decisions; do not force every vertex to that one height.

## Described form versus interpretive options

The Temple Institute's English illustrated tour describes youthful boy-and-girl faces. It also treats Shlomo's larger figures separately. The page is an illustrated interpretation, not an authenticated photograph of the ancient kaporet or a measured scan. [Temple Institute: Holy of Holies](https://templeinstitute.org/illustrated-tour-the-holy-of-holies/).

The Institute's Hebrew keruvim article acknowledges incomplete biblical anatomical detail. It attributes birdlike bodies with youthful faces to Rashbam, and a human-like head/shoulders with a birdlike lower body and wings to Rabbi Avraham ben HaRambam; it notes that the legs' form is not specified in that description. These are attributed commentary options, not permission to combine any fantasy-creature features. [Wikimikdash: keruvim, form and sources 6–7](https://wiki.temple.org.il/wt/כרובים). The direct Rashbam page was located but returned no readable commentary in this audit; that attribution was checked through the Institute article, not independently from the full commentary text.

Three workable production choices:

- **Selected Institute visual interpretation:** create original sculpted figures matching a clearly identified Institute kaporet illustration's composition, with youthful faces and raised wings. Label it as that illustration's interpretation. Obtain a usable license before incorporating the illustration itself. Do not claim the model copies an extant Temple Institute sacred Aron without identifying such an object.
- **Commentary-led hybrid bird form:** use the Institute-attributed human head/shoulders and birdlike lower form. Keep the body wing-covered and sculptural. This offers an explicit anatomical rationale and avoids adding an invented adult human costume. Cite the selected commentary attribution on the asset record.
- **Conservative silhouette study:** model two clearly winged, youthful-faced forms with simplified anatomy, validate positioning and clear space, then commission/refine detailed anatomy from the chosen interpretation. This is useful production work, but it remains a study and cannot pass final realism review simply because it has wings.

Recommended next step: use the **commentary-led form for an original review sculpt**, with Institute material as a comparison reference and a clearly recorded profile. Avoid starting from a generic Western angel asset. The source does not require a halo, harp, adult muscular torso, Roman dress or decorative pedestal; these would add unsupported visual language. A gold sculpture's birdlike body/wing coverage can provide a modest presentation without inventing exposed human anatomy.

## Concrete geometry brief for the next artist

1. Keep the current 125 × 75 cm kaporet footprint and the selected scale profile. Reassess the existing ±40 cm X anchors as provisional composition aids: they are not source measurements. Place one figure at each end with both roots visually continuous with the cover, not standing on added plinths.
2. Establish two source-tagged head/face landmarks looking inward and slightly downward. Build readable brows, nose, cheeks and lips as restrained gold relief/sculpture. Do not impose precise eye size, hair style or a modern child's likeness as textual fact. The face-measure datum is a minimum from the cited discussion, not an exact portrait blueprint.
3. Give each form a coherent pair of raised wings as the selected artistic implementation. Arrange them to shelter the central cover area while leaving a legible space between the figures. Start with broad wing masses, then layer feather groups. Exact feather count, primary-feather shape, wing span and curvature are artistic dimensions and must be stored as such.
4. Use the ten-tefach clearance datum to evaluate the relationship among cover, heads and sheltering wings. Record the actual minimum central clear height and the chosen interpretation of that datum. Do not confuse total object height with clear space beneath wings or silently include the thickness of the cover in the ten-tefach gap.
5. Keep the pair static in this first version. Different poses, miraculous movement, interpersonal attachment or service-dependent changes are a separate source-and-animation decision; this brief does not authorize an invented behavior loop.
6. Create editable component groups for left/right figure, major wing forms and detail, but make the rendered root transitions continuous with the kaporet. Separate digital authoring parts do not purport to reproduce the physical manufacturing method. Produce a joined review/export version without floating attachments or visible manufactured bolts.
7. Preserve the existing Aron's geometry version and import the review into a new namespace. Validate outward normals, watertight major forms, valid UVs, exact imported bounds and material slots. The previous OBJ handedness and UV lessons apply: native verification must precede placement.

## Decisions that remain artistic or unresolved

Precise anatomy below the shoulders; stance and feet; youthful age cues; face width versus height interpretation; male/female visual differentiation; hair; number and organization of feather rows; exact wing span; root positions; thickness and curvature of wings; ornamental surface treatment; degree of symmetry; exact correspondence to any particular Institute illustration. Do not fill these fields with unsupported centimeter values and label them halacha.

The reviewed sources support moving beyond empty anchors. They do not support describing an attractive sculpt as the exact future keruvim. Keep the asset metadata in three groups: **textual constraint**, **chosen interpretation**, and **artist-authored dimension**.

## Acceptance before integration

- Source review identifies the selected anatomical and scale profiles and confirms that kaporet, Shlomo and wall-cherub features were not mixed.
- Front, side, rear and three-quarter views show exactly two coherent figures, inward/downward faces, raised sheltering wings and continuous roots. No clipping, floating feathers, disconnected wings or generic angel props.
- Measure the body/cover/clear-space datums independently. Record the modeled dimensions, rather than declaring compliance from an overall bounding box alone.
- Close gold shading keeps faces and feather layers readable; wide views preserve a dignified silhouette and the Aron's focal role. Surface polish cannot compensate for weak sculptural anatomy.
- Native import reports no tangent/UV failures; a packaged view verifies actual visibility. A source document, reserve anchor, imported primitive or shader-success log cannot mark the keruvim complete.

No assets, native scripts or maps were edited during this brief; no protected image was downloaded or incorporated.
